02 · Transparent score

A clear baseline, with the working shown.

The Student Success Score is a 0–100 weighted average of five normalized indicators. Advisors can inspect each student's weighted point contributions.

Score = 0.35 × academics + 0.20 × attendance + 0.15 × LMS + 0.15 × engagement/skills + 0.15 × placement readiness
Academic performance35%
Attendance20%
LMS activity15%
Engagement & skills15%
Placement readiness15%

Academics = CGPA ÷ 4 × 100; engagement/skills = mean of the two indices. Placement measures are pathway-relevant: coding for Computer Science, and domain and pathway-specific assessments for other programs. Example Business placements include marketing/sales, finance/accounting, and HR/operations; Psychology includes HR, research assistance, and community outreach; Engineering includes quality, production planning, and CAD/design. Coding is optional and not used for non-IT students. Suggested roles and skill examples are synthetic, not verified qualifications or job openings. Backlogs provide context. Risk: High below 50; Elevated below 65 or if attendance <75%, LMS <45, or placement <50 with CGPA ≥3.2; otherwise On track.

04 · Explainable, not a black box

Show what moved the score—and why a flag appeared.

Example: a student with strong academics and placement readiness below the defined threshold. The profile ranks score contributions and gives the exact risk reason.

Illustrative profile · Business 70 / 100

Attendance · 20%17.4 pts
LMS · 15%10.2 pts
Engagement & skills · 15%6.8 pts
Placement readiness · 15%4.3 pts
Risk explanation

Elevated · placement priority

Placement readiness is 29/100, below the 50 threshold, while CGPA is 3.5. This triggers a placement-focused support flag.

Possible next step
Offer a placement-readiness session or skills coaching. Review context with the student; the flag is a prompt, not a decision.

Contribution values are normalized indicator × weight and sum to the displayed score after rounding.

07 · Demo flow & responsible use

From insight to a thoughtful human action.

01 · Filter

Choose a program, risk level, or a targeted support segment.

02 · Understand

Open a profile to inspect source indicators, score contributions, and risk reasons.

03 · Support

Agree on a next step with the student and log the intervention.

04 · Review

Use cohort trends and CSV export to support team follow-up.

Prototype limits

All 36 profiles and detailed indicator values are synthetic. Historical trend points and intervention totals are illustrative. The rules-based score is not a trained AI model or validated prediction.

Before real deployment

Integrate approved SIS/LMS sources; validate data and fairness; add an institutional identity provider, role-based access, privacy controls, audit logs, and outcome evaluation.

Use the score to guide a conversation—not to define a student.
